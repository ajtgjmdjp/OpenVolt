# Capital Gains Tax Rules: Japan and United States

Verified: 2026-03-19
All facts verified against primary government sources.

---

## Japan (日本)

### 1. 課税期間 (Tax Year / Calculation Period)

**1月1日〜12月31日**

所得税の確定申告は、毎年1月1日から12月31日までの1年間に生じた所得について行う。損益通算もこの暦年単位で計算される。

- Source: [No.2020 確定申告｜国税庁](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/2020.htm)

### 2. 確定申告の期限

- **申告期間**: 翌年2月16日〜3月15日（原則）
- **還付申告**: 翌年1月1日から提出可能（確定申告期間前でも可）
- **還付申告の期限**: 翌年1月1日から**5年間**

- Source: [No.2020 確定申告｜国税庁](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/2020.htm)
- Source: [No.2030 還付申告｜国税庁](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/2030.htm)

### 3. 税率

上場株式等の譲渡所得に対する税率（申告分離課税）:

| 税目 | 税率 |
|---|---|
| 所得税 | 15% |
| 住民税 | 5% |
| 復興特別所得税 | 所得税額の2.1%（= 0.315%） |
| **合計** | **20.315%** |

復興特別所得税は2013年〜2037年の期間適用。

- Source: [No.1463 株式等を譲渡したときの課税（申告分離課税）｜国税庁](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1463.htm)

### 4. 特定口座（源泉徴収あり）での処理

- 証券会社が年間の譲渡損益を計算し、譲渡益に対して**15.315%（所得税+復興特別所得税）+住民税5%**を源泉徴収
- 同一口座内で譲渡損失がある場合、配当等の金額から損失を控除した上で源泉徴収額を計算（口座内での自動損益通算）
- 源泉徴収選択の届出は「その年の最初の譲渡の時まで」に提出。選択は年単位（年途中変更不可）
- 源泉徴収口座の場合、申告不要を選択可能（ただし損益通算・繰越控除を受ける場合は確定申告が必要）

- Source: [No.1476 特定口座制度｜国税庁](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1476.htm)

### 5. 損益通算と繰越控除

- **損益通算**: 上場株式等の譲渡損失は、上場株式等の配当所得等（申告分離課税を選択したもの）と通算可能
- **繰越控除**: 通算しきれなかった損失は翌年以降**3年間**繰越可能
- **制限事項**:
  - 上場株式等の損失と一般株式等の譲渡益は通算不可（逆も同様）
  - NISA口座内の損失は損益通算・繰越控除の対象外
  - 繰越控除を受けるには、損失が生じた年から**毎年連続して**確定申告が必要（取引がない年も）

- Source: [No.1474 上場株式等に係る譲渡損失の損益通算及び繰越控除｜国税庁](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1474.htm)
- Source: [No.1465 株式等の譲渡損失（赤字）の取扱い｜国税庁](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1465.htm)

### 6. 還付のタイミング

- **e-Tax（電子申告）**: 約3週間で処理
- **書面申告**: おおむね1か月〜1か月半程度

- Source: [税金の還付 Q&A｜国税庁](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/shinkoku/qa/11.htm)
- Source: [還付金処理状況確認｜e-Tax](https://www.e-tax.nta.go.jp/kakunin/kanpukinsyori.htm)

## United States

### 1. Tax Year

**January 1 through December 31** (calendar year for most individual taxpayers).

- Source: [Topic No. 409, Capital Gains and Losses｜IRS](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc409)

### 2. Short-Term vs Long-Term Capital Gains

| Holding Period | Classification | Tax Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **1 year or less** | Short-term | Taxed as ordinary income (10%-37%) |
| **More than 1 year** | Long-term | Preferential rates (0%, 15%, or 20%) |

Holding period is measured from the day after acquisition through the date of disposition.

- Source: [Topic No. 409, Capital Gains and Losses｜IRS](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc409)

### 3. Long-Term Capital Gains Tax Rates (Tax Year 2025)

| Filing Status | 0% Rate | 15% Rate | 20% Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single | up to $48,350 | $48,351 - $533,400 | over $533,400 |
| Married Filing Jointly | up to $96,700 | $96,701 - $600,050 | over $600,050 |

Additionally, the **3.8% Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT)** applies to the lesser of net investment income or MAGI exceeding:
- Single / Head of Household: $200,000
- Married Filing Jointly: $250,000
- Married Filing Separately: $125,000

Effective maximum rate on long-term capital gains: **23.8%** (20% + 3.8% NIIT).

- Source: [Topic No. 409, Capital Gains and Losses｜IRS](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc409)
- Source: [Net Investment Income Tax｜IRS](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/net-investment-income-tax)

### 4. Capital Loss Deduction and Carryforward

- Capital losses first offset capital gains (ST vs ST, then LT vs LT, then cross-category)
- Excess capital losses offset up to **$3,000** of ordinary income per year ($1,500 if married filing separately)
- Losses beyond the $3,000 annual limit carry forward **indefinitely** (no expiration)

- Source: [Topic No. 409｜IRS](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc409)
- Source: [Publication 550 (2025)｜IRS](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550)

### 5. Wash Sale Rule (IRC Section 1091)

- **Window**: 61-day period — 30 days before through 30 days after the sale date
- **Trigger**: Selling at a loss and acquiring "substantially identical" securities within this window
- **Effect**: Loss is **disallowed** (added to cost basis of replacement, deferring deduction)
- **"Substantially identical"**: Same company shares = yes; different companies in same industry = generally no; same-index ETFs = may be
- **Exception**: Does not apply to dealers in ordinary course of business

- Source: [Publication 550 (2025), Wash Sales｜IRS](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550)

### 6. Tax-Loss Harvesting

Not separately codified — a strategy within existing rules:
- Sell at a loss to realize capital loss
- Use loss to offset gains or up to $3,000 ordinary income
- Purchase non-substantially-identical security to maintain exposure
- Must respect wash sale 30-day window
- Unused harvested losses carry forward indefinitely

- Source: [Publication 550｜IRS](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p550)
- Source: [Schedule D Instructions｜IRS](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040sd)

## Comparison Summary

| Item | Japan | United States |
|---|---|---|
| Tax year | Jan 1 - Dec 31 | Jan 1 - Dec 31 |
| Tax rate on gains | 20.315% (flat) | 0-23.8% (progressive) |
| Short/long distinction | No | Yes (1-year threshold) |
| Loss offset limit/year | No dollar limit (same category) | $3,000 against ordinary income |
| Loss carryforward | 3 years | Indefinite |
| Wash sale rule | None | 30 days before/after |
| Withholding at source | Available (特定口座) | Not typical |
| Filing deadline | Feb 16 - Mar 15 | April 15 |
